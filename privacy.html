Privacy Policy

This policy describes how CUT LINE handles information in version 1.0 of the iOS app.

Summary

CUT LINE does not collect personal data from users. The game does not require an account, login, or profile, and normal gameplay is designed to work offline.

Data collection

CUT LINE does not collect, store, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information through the app. Version 1.0 does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

Local game data

Game settings, unlocks, scores, and progression may be stored locally on your device so the game can remember your progress. This local game data is not transmitted to the developer by CUT LINE.

Device permissions

CUT LINE does not require access to your camera, microphone, photo library, contacts, location, or advertising identifier for normal gameplay.

Apple services

Downloads, purchases, device backups, crash diagnostics, or other interactions performed by Apple or the App Store may be processed by Apple according to Apple's own terms and privacy policies. Those services are separate from data collection by CUT LINE itself.

Children's privacy

Because CUT LINE does not collect personal information through the app, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the game.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of CUT LINE adds online services or changes its data practices, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before those changes are released.

Support

Questions about the game can be directed through the developer contact associated with the CUT LINE App Store listing. Technical help is also available on the Support page.
