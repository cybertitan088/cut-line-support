Support

Help for CUT LINE on iPhone. The game is designed for landscape play and does not require an account or an internet connection for normal gameplay.

How to play

  • Hold anywhere on the gameplay surface to begin charging.
  • Drag while holding to rotate the projected slash arc.
  • Release to execute one slash through the displayed arc.
  • Waiting longer can line up more enemies, but waiting too long can end the run.

If the game is not behaving correctly

  • Close CUT LINE completely and reopen it.
  • Make sure the device is being held in landscape orientation.
  • Restart the current Line from the pause menu if a run appears stuck.
  • Restart the iPhone if touch input or orientation remains incorrect after reopening the game.
  • Install the newest available CUT LINE update from the App Store before reporting an issue.

Save data

CUT LINE stores progress locally on the device. Deleting the app may also delete locally stored progress unless the operating system preserves it as part of a device backup.

Accounts, purchases, and connectivity

CUT LINE does not require an account or sign-in. Version 1.0 does not require a network connection for campaign play and does not use advertising or loot boxes. The in-game Teeth shop uses gameplay-earned currency and does not require a real-money purchase.

Privacy

For details about data handling, read the CUT LINE Privacy Policy.

When reporting a problem through the developer contact shown with the App Store listing, include your iPhone model, iOS version, CUT LINE version, and a short description of what happened.
